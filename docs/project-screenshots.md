The PNG archives preserve the exact supplied files. Website copies retain their original dimensions and use WebP quality 0.96 to reduce the combined transfer from about 6.2 MB to 0.96 MB. Presentation uses contain sizing and unfiltered colors, so the complete screenshot remains visible in the card and detail dialog. Descriptive filenames prevent previously cached concept artwork from replacing the new screenshots.

Small Hours and DREADRAIL retain their existing artwork. `content.js` supplies image paths, real dimensions, alternative text, and screenshot presentation metadata to static output, browser cards, detail dialogs, and search structured data.
