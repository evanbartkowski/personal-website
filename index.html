I work in cybersecurity. I think in systems. And I never really stop building.

I'm Evan, a Cybersecurity Operations Implementation Engineer at CyberMaxx. I deploy and tune security solutions, automate defensive workflows, and help organizations build more resilient environments.

I graduated from UMBC in May 2025 with a computer science degree on the Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning track. Outside work, that curiosity takes me into reverse engineering, Linux internals, AI experiments, and interactive worlds. The common thread: understand the system, question the assumptions, and make something that holds up under pressure.
