01 / THE HUMAN BEHIND THE TERMINAL

A builder's mind.
A wanderer's curiosity.

I like the space where solid engineering meets something a little unexpected.

I'm Evan. I build web applications, native software, and interactive worlds. My projects range from making fight data easier to explore to creating entire lives and unsettling train rides.

The thread through all of it is the same: understand the system, care about the details, and make the experience feel human. I enjoy the hard parts as much as the finishing touches.
